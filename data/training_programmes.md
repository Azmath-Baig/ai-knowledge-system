# Training Programmes

## 1. Mandatory Training

All employees must complete information security, data protection and health and safety training within their first month, and refresh it every year. Mandatory courses are assigned automatically in the Learning Portal.

## 2. Induction Programme

New starters complete a two-week induction programme covering company values, systems, and an introduction to each department. Each new starter is assigned a buddy for their first three months.

## 3. Professional Development Budget

Each employee has a learning budget of £1,000 per year for courses, certifications and conferences relevant to their role. Spending from the budget must be approved by your manager before booking. If you leave within 12 months of completing a course costing more than £500, you may be asked to repay part of the cost.

## 4. Study Leave

Employees studying for an approved professional qualification can take up to five days of paid study leave per year for exams and exam preparation.

## 5. Learning Portal

The Learning Portal provides free online courses on technical, leadership and personal skills. You can access it from the intranet using your normal login.
