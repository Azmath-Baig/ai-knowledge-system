# Career Development

## 1. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews take place twice a year, in June and December. Each review includes a self-assessment, feedback from your manager, and agreed objectives for the next six months.

## 2. Promotions

Promotions are considered during the December review cycle. Your manager will discuss your readiness against the competency framework for your next level. Promotions take effect from 1 January.

## 3. Internal Job Applications

Internal vacancies are advertised on the intranet before they are advertised externally. Employees who have been in their current role for at least 12 months can apply; you should let your manager know before applying.

## 4. Mentoring Scheme

Any employee can join the mentoring scheme to be paired with a more senior colleague from a different department. Applications open each quarter through the HR portal.

## 5. Career Conversations

In addition to formal reviews, employees are encouraged to have a career conversation with their manager at least once a quarter to discuss goals, skills and progression.
