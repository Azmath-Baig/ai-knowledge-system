# Working Arrangements

## 1. Core Working Hours

Standard working hours are 37.5 hours per week, Monday to Friday. Core hours, when all employees are expected to be available, are 10:00 to 16:00. Outside core hours, employees may start and finish at times that suit them, provided their team's needs are met.

## 2. Remote Working

Employees may work remotely on Fridays where this has been agreed with their manager. Remote working on other days is available for up to two days per week in total, including Friday, subject to manager approval. Employees in client-facing roles may have different arrangements set out in their contract.

When working from home, employees must use a NALITS-issued laptop, connect through the company VPN, and work from a private space where confidential conversations cannot be overheard.

## 3. Office Attendance

Employees are expected to attend the office at least three days per week. Team days are set by each department and attendance on team days is expected unless agreed otherwise with your manager.

## 4. Flexible Working Requests

Any employee with at least 26 weeks of service can make a formal flexible working request, for example to change their hours, work compressed hours or permanently change their working location. Requests are submitted through the HR portal and will receive a decision within 4 weeks.

## 5. Working Abroad

Working from another country, even temporarily, requires written approval from HR and your manager at least 30 days in advance because of tax and data protection implications. Working abroad is limited to a maximum of 20 working days per calendar year.
