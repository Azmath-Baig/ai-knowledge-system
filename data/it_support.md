# IT Support

## 1. Reporting a Problem

To report a laptop problem or any other IT issue, raise a ticket on the IT Service Desk portal or email servicedesk@nalits.example. For urgent issues that stop you working, call the IT Service Desk on extension 4000. Please include your asset number, which is printed on a sticker on the underside of your laptop.

## 2. Support Hours

The IT Service Desk is available Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00. Critical outages outside these hours can be reported to the on-call engineer through the out-of-hours number on the intranet.

## 3. Passwords and Account Access

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are changed every 90 days. If you are locked out of your account, use the self-service password reset tool or contact the Service Desk. Never share your password with anyone, including IT staff.

## 4. Equipment and Software

Each employee is issued a laptop, a monitor and a headset. Additional equipment must be requested through the Service Desk with manager approval. Only software approved by IT may be installed; requests for new software are reviewed within five working days.

## 5. Lost or Stolen Devices

Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the Service Desk immediately, and in any case within 24 hours, so the device can be locked and wiped remotely. This is required under our data protection obligations.
