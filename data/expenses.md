# Expenses Policy

## 1. What You Can Claim

Employees can claim reasonable expenses that are incurred wholly for business purposes. This includes travel to client sites, approved training courses, business accommodation, and meals while travelling on company business. Travel between home and your normal place of work cannot be claimed.

## 2. Travel

Standard class rail travel should be booked through the company travel portal. Mileage for using your own car on business journeys can be claimed at 45p per mile. Taxis may be claimed only where public transport is unavailable or unsafe, for example late at night.

## 3. Meals and Accommodation

When travelling on business, meals can be claimed up to £30 per day. Hotel accommodation should be booked through the travel portal and should not exceed £150 per night outside London or £200 per night in London. Alcohol cannot be claimed.

## 4. How to Submit a Claim

Expense claims are submitted through the finance system within 60 days of the expense being incurred. Every claim must include an itemised receipt. Claims are reviewed by your manager and reimbursed with the next monthly salary payment.

## 5. Non-Claimable Items

Personal items, fines (including parking and speeding fines), upgrades to first class, and entertainment that has not been approved in advance cannot be claimed.
