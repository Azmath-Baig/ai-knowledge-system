# Employee Policies

## 1. Code of Conduct

All employees are expected to act with honesty, respect and professionalism towards colleagues, clients and partners. Harassment, bullying and discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated and may result in disciplinary action.

## 2. Sickness Absence

If you are unwell and cannot work, tell your manager before 10:00 on your first day of absence. For absences of more than seven calendar days, a fit note from your doctor is required. Employees receive full pay for up to 10 days of sickness absence per year.

## 3. Probation Period

New employees have a six-month probation period. Your manager will hold check-in meetings at three and six months, and your employment is confirmed in writing at the end of a successful probation.

## 4. Raising a Concern

If you have a concern about wrongdoing, you can raise it with your manager, HR, or anonymously through the whistleblowing hotline. Employees who raise a concern in good faith will be protected from any negative treatment.

## 5. Notice Period

After probation, the notice period is one month for employees and three months for managers, unless your contract states otherwise. During probation, the notice period is one week.
