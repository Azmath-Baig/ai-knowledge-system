# Holiday Policy

## 1. Annual Leave Entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 days of annual leave per year, in addition to public holidays. Entitlement increases by one day for each full year of service, up to a maximum of 30 days. Part-time employees receive a pro-rata entitlement based on their contracted hours.

The holiday year runs from 1 January to 31 December.

## 2. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to all UK public holidays (bank holidays) in addition to their annual leave. If you are required to work on a public holiday, you will receive a day off in lieu, to be taken within three months.

## 3. Booking Leave

Annual leave must be requested through the HR portal and approved by your manager before it is taken. Requests for more than five consecutive days should be made at least four weeks in advance. Managers will try to approve requests on a first-come, first-served basis while maintaining adequate cover.

## 4. Carrying Over Leave

Up to five days of unused annual leave can be carried over into the next holiday year. Carried-over days must be used by 31 March, after which they are lost. Exceptions may be made where leave could not be taken because of sickness.

## 5. Office Closure Period

The office closes between Christmas and New Year. Employees are required to reserve three days of their annual leave entitlement for this closure period.
